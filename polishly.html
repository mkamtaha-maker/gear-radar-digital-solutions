Questions

Will you make things up to impress employers?

No. We only use what is in your CV and what you tell us. We rewrite it so your real experience reads clearly and strongly.

What is an ATS-friendly CV?

Many companies scan CVs with software (Applicant Tracking Systems). We use clear headings, standard sections and the keywords from your target job so the software can read it.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. You get two free edit rounds within 7 days. Just write what you want changed in plain words.

What happens to my CV?

We use it only to make your new CV and delete the files after 30 days.
