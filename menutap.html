How MenuTap works

  1. Choose a plan and a design

    Pick Starter, Pro or Premium, then choose a theme and a layout from our QR menu gallery.

  2. Send your menu online

    Add your dishes, prices, descriptions and photos in one online form. You can also tell us how many tables you have.

  3. We build and check it

    We build your menu, check it and send you your QR code and printable table cards, usually within a few days.

  4. Customers scan and order

    Customers scan the QR code at the table. You change prices whenever you like with your own edit code.

Who MenuTap is for

Restaurants, cafés, takeaways, bakeries, dessert shops, bars and food trucks, and also salons and shops that want a price list or product catalogue behind a QR code. We are based in Greater Manchester and work remotely with businesses across the UK, Ireland, the USA and Canada.

Questions restaurant owners ask

How much does a QR code menu cost?

QR Starter is £30, QR Pro is £50 and QR Premium is £70, paid once. These are launch prices, 50% off until 31 January 2027. Optional monthly menu updates are £9 a month.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and the menu opens in their browser.

Can I change my prices myself?

Yes. Every plan comes with an edit code, so you can change prices any time without reprinting anything.

Can customers order from the table?

Yes, on QR Pro and QR Premium. Customers add dishes to a cart and send the order with their table number, either to your live orders screen or to your WhatsApp.

Do I get a different QR code for each table?

On QR Pro and QR Premium, yes. Tell us how many tables you have when you sign up and you get a print page with a QR code for each table.

How long does it take?

QR menus are usually ready within a few days of receiving your menu. We check every menu before it goes live.

Can my menu be in Arabic and English?

Yes. QR Pro and QR Premium menus work in English and Arabic.

Is it only for restaurants?

No. Cafés, takeaways, salons and shops use MenuTap for price lists and product catalogues too.
