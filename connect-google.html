Connect your Google account

Tala needs your permission to work with your Google account. You will be taken to Google's own sign-in page, where you can review and approve these permissions:

  • Gmail – send email: Tala sends emails only after you confirm them. Tala cannot read your inbox.
  • Google Calendar – events: to show your day and add events you confirm.
  • Google Drive – files Tala creates: to save scans and photos you choose. Tala cannot see your other files.
Continue to Google
