Who Duo is for

Restaurants, cafés, takeaways, bakeries and shops that want to be found online and give customers a menu they can scan and order from. We are based in Greater Manchester and work remotely with businesses across the UK, Ireland, the USA and Canada.

Questions about Duo

What is Duo?

Duo is a bundle of a Webly website and a MenuTap QR menu at the same level, built together from one sign-up, for less than buying both.

How much does Duo cost?

Website + QR Starter is £35, Pro is £60 and Premium is £85, paid once. These are launch prices, 50% off until 31 January 2027. Optional monthly care for both is £20 a month.

How much do I save?

Compared with buying the website and the QR menu separately, you save £5 on Starter, £10 on Pro and £15 on Premium.

How long does it take?

Both are usually ready within a few days of receiving your details. We check both before they go live.

Can customers order from the table?

Yes, with the Pro and Premium bundles. Customers send their order with their table number to your live orders screen or WhatsApp.

Are there any monthly fees?

No. You pay once and hosting on our link is included. Monthly care is optional.
