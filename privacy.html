Privacy Notice

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This notice explains how Gear Radar Digital Solutions ("we", "us") uses personal information collected through this website, www.gearradar.co.uk. We are based in Manchester, United Kingdom, and we handle personal information in line with UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

1. Who is responsible for your data

Gear Radar Digital Solutions is the controller of the personal information described here. You can contact us about privacy at info@gearradar.co.uk.

2. What we collect

When you use our contact form we collect:

  • your name and email address;
  • your phone or WhatsApp number and business name, if you choose to give them;
  • the service you are interested in and the message you write;
  • the language you were viewing the site in and the date and time of your enquiry.

If you contact us directly by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive the details you share in that conversation.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you an automatic confirmation email.
  • To prepare a quote and, if you become a client, to provide our services.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and, where you go on to use our services, taking steps to enter into a contract with you. We do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use trusted service providers to run our business, who process data on our behalf:

  • n8n – the automation platform that receives form submissions and stores enquiries;
  • Google Workspace – our business email;
  • Telegram – used to notify our team that a new enquiry has arrived;
  • GitHub – hosts this website.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards they provide, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or equivalent protections.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client, in which case we keep records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations.

6. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email info@gearradar.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Our services

Some of our services, such as the AI Executive Assistant, connect to accounts you choose to link (for example Gmail or Google Calendar). How that data is used is set out in the service terms we give you before you connect anything. You can disconnect at any time.

8. Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
