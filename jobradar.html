Questions job seekers ask

What is JobRadar?

JobRadar is a job search service from Gear Radar. It finds jobs that match your CV every day and sends them to you on WhatsApp or Telegram, with a match score, the gaps to fix, and tailored CV points.

How much does JobRadar cost?

JobRadar costs £17 a month in total on Telegram: £10 for JobRadar plus £7 for Tala Essential, the assistant it runs inside. JobRadar Plus is £27 a month in total. On WhatsApp, with Tala Pro, it is £22 or £32 a month. These are launch prices, 50% off until 31 January 2027, and your monthly price is locked for 12 months.

Which countries does it search?

JobRadar searches job sites across the UK, the USA, Canada and the Gulf. You choose the cities, and you can include remote or hybrid roles.

Does it apply for jobs for me?

No. JobRadar finds and scores the jobs and prepares tailored CV points and cover letters. You stay in control and apply yourself, using the link in each match.

Will it make up experience on my CV?

Never. Every CV point and cover letter is written only from the experience in your own CV.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Everything happens in WhatsApp or Telegram, which you already use.

Can I try JobRadar for free?

Yes. Add JobRadar to Tala Essential (monthly) and you get a 3-day free trial of both. You add a card at sign-up but nothing is charged during the trial. We remind you a day before the first payment, and if you cancel before the trial ends you pay nothing. One trial per person.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel any time from inside Tala, with no contract.
