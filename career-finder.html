How the career finder works

1. You describe your real work

Not your job title, your tasks. The skills hidden in your daily work are what employers in other fields pay for.

2. We match it to real roles

We look at which roles use those same skills, what you want next and the job market in your country.

3. You get a clear plan

4 directions with job titles to search for, why you fit, and one cheap first step for each one.

Questions about the career finder

Is the career finder free?

Yes. It is free, with no card and no sign-up. We ask for your email only to send you a copy of your results.

Who is it for?

Anyone thinking about their next move: people stuck in a role, people who want less physical work or better pay, career changers, graduates and people moving to the UK or the Gulf.

Are the results personal?

Yes. They are based only on what you write. The more detail you give about your daily tasks, the better the directions.

What do I do with the results?

Search for the job titles on job sites, update your CV and LinkedIn with the skills listed, and take the first step. JobRadar can send you new jobs for these titles every day by email.

Can I use it more than once?

Yes, up to 3 times a day with the same email, so you can try different answers.
