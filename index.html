Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We handle the setup and show you how everything works. If you can use WhatsApp, you will be fine.

How much does it cost?

Each service has its own page with full prices. Websites and QR menus are paid once, and monthly services can be cancelled at any time. Current prices include a 50% launch discount until 31 January 2027.

Is my data safe?

We only use your details to provide the service you asked for. We never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. See our privacy notice.

How long does setup take?

Websites and QR menus are usually ready within a few days of receiving your details. For anything else we give you a timeline with the price.

Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Yes. Everything is set up remotely, so we can work with you wherever you are.
