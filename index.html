Frequently asked questions

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We do the setup. If you can use WhatsApp or Telegram, you can use what we build.

How much does it cost?

The assistant and job hunter are monthly plans from $9. Websites start at $149 and QR menus at $79, paid once. See Pricing for everything included.

Is my data safe?

The assistant only accesses the accounts you choose to connect, and asks before taking any action on your behalf. You can disconnect at any time.

How long does setup take?

Websites and QR catalogues are usually ready within a few days of receiving your details. We'll give you a timeline for other services when we quote.

Do you work with clients outside Manchester?

Yes. Everything is set up remotely, so we can work with you wherever you are.
