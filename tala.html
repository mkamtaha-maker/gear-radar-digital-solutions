Questions people ask about Tala

What is Tala?

Tala is a personal assistant from Gear Radar that works inside Telegram or WhatsApp. She writes and sends your emails from Gmail after you confirm, manages your Google Calendar, sets reminders and, on Tala Pro, sends you a daily morning brief.

How much does Tala cost?

Tala Essential is £7 a month and Tala Pro is £12 a month. These are launch prices, 50% off until 31 January 2027, and your monthly price stays locked for 12 months. Annual billing gives you 2 months free.

Can I try Tala for free?

Yes. Tala Essential (monthly) comes with a 3-day free trial, and you can add JobRadar to it. You add a card at sign-up but nothing is charged during the trial. We remind you a day before the first payment, and if you cancel before the trial ends you pay nothing. One trial per person.

Does Tala read my emails?

No. Tala uses Gmail only to send emails you have approved. She writes the draft, shows it to you, and sends it only after you tap Send.

Can I use Tala on WhatsApp?

Yes, with Tala Pro. Tala Essential works on Telegram.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Tala works inside Telegram or WhatsApp, which you already use.

Does Tala speak Arabic?

Yes. You can talk to Tala in English or Arabic, by text or, on Tala Pro, by voice note.

Is my data safe?

Tala only accesses the accounts you choose to connect, and asks before taking any action on your behalf. You can disconnect at any time.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel any time from inside Tala, with no contract.
